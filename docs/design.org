** The dataset

#+begin_example
runs: 1, 2                 T0 = baselines (eyes open, eyes closed)
runs: 3, 4, 7, 8, 11, 12   T1 = left fist, T2 = right fist
runs: 5, 6, 9, 10, 13, 14  T1 = both fists, T2 = both feet
#+end_example

Runs 1 and 2 (T0) are completely ignored in classification for two reasons:

1. T0 is not a condition, it's the absence of one. A rest-vs-task classifier is a different (and much easier) problem than left-vs-right.

2. CSP is a two-class method. =fit= solves $S_0 w = \mu (S_0 + S_1) w$ - a generalised eigenproblem on exactly two scatter matrices.

The whole dataset was recorded with 64 channels on BCI2000. The EDF channel names are not standard, which is what =eegbci.standardize= fixes. Four subjects were recorded at 128 Hz rather than 160 Hz; nothing on the training path depends on the rate, and the only place it surfaces is the =fmax= clip in =plot_psd_comparison=.

** Why the group, not the run

=runs_for= expands a run number to the three runs of its =BASE_GROUPS= entry: =3= -> =[3, 7, 11]=. T1 and T2 mean one thing across those three, which is what makes =concatenate_raws= legal. Concatenating =3= with =5= would redefine T1 from "left fist" to "both fists" and produce labels that no longer name one problem.

The other reason is arithmetic: one run holds ~15 T1/T2 events, so its 20% holdout is 3 epochs. Three runs give ~45 epochs, ~22 per class, ~9 held out. The group is the smallest unit a stratified split means anything on.

The pooled experiments are the same argument at six runs — executed and imagined denote identical movements. =runs_for= searches =BASE_GROUPS= only: a run number cannot tell its base group from the pooled experiment containing it.

** Preprocessing decisions

*The band is 7-30 Hz.* Mu (8-13) and beta (13-30) are the rhythms that desynchronise over sensorimotor cortex during movement and imagery, so they carry the class difference CSP is looking for. Below mu is drift and ocular artefact, above beta is EMG and line noise, and neither separates left from right. The low edge sits at 7 rather than 8 so the transition band falls outside mu instead of eating its lower half.

*The window is 1-2 s post-onset.* The first second after cue onset is dominated by the evoked response to the cue itself, which is common to both classes and therefore useless to a discriminative method. ERD develops over the second that follows, so that is the second we keep. One second is also enough samples that the per-epoch contribution to the concatenated covariance is not degenerate.

*No re-referencing.* CSP is invariant under invertible maps of the channel axis, so a re-reference is a no-op at best. CAR is worse: it drops =S_0 + S_1= to rank $C-1$, and the Cholesky step inside =eigh= ceases to exist. The failure is silent — eigenvalues outside $[0,1]$ and a =complex128= filter matrix, no exception. See "Why no re-referencing" in [[file:csp.org][csp.org]].

*The band-pass licenses the covariance estimator.* =_covariance= never centres. That is only correct because the 7-30 Hz filter has no DC gain, so the channel means are already ~0. Dropping the high-pass would invalidate the estimator, not just degrade it. See "Zero-mean assumption".

*The filter is acausal.* =firwin= is zero-phase: each output sample depends on input both before and after it. Right for offline analysis, impossible in a deployed causal buffer. Not leakage — the filter is fit to nothing and sees no labels. It does mean =predict= measures per-chunk classification cost, not real-time latency.

** Where the leakage boundary is

=fit_pipeline= splits =idx=, not arrays. =X= and =y= stay whole and every stage addresses rows by index, so no copy can drift out of sync with its labels.

=MyCSP= sits inside the =Pipeline=, so =clf.fit(X[train_idx], ...)= estimates =S_0=, =S_1= and =filters_= on training rows only. That is the whole guarantee: CSP fitted outside the pipeline would see all 45 epochs and inflate every number here. =cross_val_score= clones the pipeline and refits CSP per fold, and it only ever receives =X[train_idx]= — the holdout is carved out before it runs.

=train= persists =test_idx= with the =PreprocConfig= it was fitted under, and =predict= reloads under that config rather than the current default. A later edit to =PreprocConfig= cannot silently re-score an old model on different epochs.

** Which number is the accuracy

1. *=train= -> =scores.mean()=.* Mean over 10 validation folds *inside* the training partition. Never touches =test_idx=. A fit-quality number, not a generalisation estimate.
2. *=predict=.* One accuracy on the ~9 epochs of =test_idx= at =FitConfig.seed=. One draw, quantised to 1/9.
3. *A sweep cell.* Mean of =N_REPEATS= holdouts at seeds derived from =(fit.seed, subject, repeat)=. Quantised to 1/45.

The 60% claim rests on 3 alone — mean over 109 subjects, then over six experiments; see [[file:../README.md][README.md]] and [[file:../results/][results/]]. So =mybci.py 4 14 predict= will not reproduce that cell: same pipeline and epochs, different splits.

* Builtins

** argparse

Builds the CLI of =mybci.py=: flags, constraints, and the usage errors raised after the =SUBJECTS= and =runs_for= checks.

** sys

Feeds =sys.argv[1:]= into =main()=.

** time

Brackets each per-chunk prediction in =score_stream=, which is what makes the <2 s latency claim measurable rather than asserted.

** dataclasses

Parameter records, so defaults live in one declaration instead of scattered call sites.

- =dataclass=: Declares =PreprocConfig= and =FitConfig=, both of which carry values only, no behaviour.

** pathlib

Every filesystem location in the project is a =Path=, never a string.

- =Path=: Builds =MODELS_DIR= and the per-group model file in =model_path=. Also the =path= threaded into =load_raw= and the existence check in =predict=.

* Externals

** mne

The EEG layer. Everything in [[file:../src/data.py][data.py]] that is not arithmetic comes from here.

- =Epochs=: Cuts =filtered= into the =tmin=/=tmax= window around each event in =make_epochs=, and is the object =load_dataset= hands back for figures.
- =pick_types=: Restricts to EEG channels before epoching, dropping everything the montage does not describe.
- =channels.make_standard_montage=: Attaches the electrode positions named by =PreprocConfig.montage=.
- =datasets.eegbci=: Resolves subject and run numbers to local EDF paths, fetching on first use, and standardises channel names.
- =io.read_raw_edf=: Reads one run file into a =Raw=.
- =io.concatenate_raws=: Joins the three runs of a group into a single =Raw=.

** numpy

Array backend throughout; the covariance reshape and the pseudo-inverse in [[file:../src/csp.py][csp.py]] are plain numpy.

- =typing=: =NDArray= annotations on the arrays that cross module boundaries — =X=, =y=, features.

** scipy

Used for exactly one call, but that call is the algorithm.

- =linalg.eigh=: Solves the generalised problem =eigh(S_0, S_0 + S_1)= in =MyCSP.fit=, returning the joint diagonaliser as eigenvectors.

** sklearn

Supplies the estimator contract =MyCSP= implements, the classifier behind it, and the splitting machinery.

- =base.BaseEstimator=: Gives =MyCSP= =get_params=/=set_params=; cross-validation clones estimators through these, so without it =cross_val_score= cannot re-instantiate the step.
- =base.TransformerMixin=: Supplies =fit_transform=, which is what =Pipeline= calls on intermediate steps.
- =discriminant_analysis.LinearDiscriminantAnalysis=: Terminal step of the pipeline. Being linear is precisely why the log transform in =MyCSP.transform= earns its place.
- =model_selection.StratifiedShuffleSplit=: The =cv= object in =fit_pipeline= — =n_splits= draws at =test_size=, class ratio preserved in each.
- =model_selection.cross_val_score=: Produces =scores=, the per-fold accuracies reported by =train=.
- =model_selection.train_test_split=: Splits =idx= into =train_idx= and =test_idx= under =stratify=; =test_idx= is the partition =predict= later replays.
- =pipeline.Pipeline=: Binds =MyCSP= to LDA so the spatial filters are fit on training folds only. This is the whole leakage guarantee.
- =utils.validation.check_is_fitted=: Guards =MyCSP.transform= against being called before =fit=.

** joblib

Serialises the fitted pipeline and its provenance to =model_path(...)= in =train=, and reads it back in =predict=. The only disk round trip in the project.

** matplotlib

Imported by [[file:../visualise.py][visualise.py]] only; nothing on the training path touches it.

- =pyplot=: Draws the before/after PSD panels, fed by =load_raw= (unfiltered) and =preprocess= (filtered).

* [[file:../src/experiments.py][experiments.py]]

Defines run groups. Imported by [[file:../src/sweep.py][sweep.py]], [[file:../mybci.py][mybci.py]] and [[file:../visualise.py][visualise.py]]; imports nothing.

** =BASE_GROUPS=

The four task groups, as =(runs, label)= pairs. Each group is one task repeated three times. Labels are printed by the sweep.

** =EXPERIMENTS=

The sweep's iteration order: the four base groups plus two pooled entries, each pairing an executed group with its imagined counterpart.

** =SUBJECTS=

The subject range. Both CLIs validate against it and =run_full_sweep()= iterates it.

** =runs_for(run)=

Expands a run to its group: =3= -> =[3, 7, 11]=. Searches =BASE_GROUPS= only, and raises =ValueError= on anything else. Called by [[file:../mybci.py][mybci.py]] and [[file:../visualise.py][visualise.py]].

* [[file:../src/data.py][data.py]]

Where the data comes from.

** =PreprocConfig=

Default preprocessing parameters.

- =l_freq=: 7 Hz, low edge of the band
- =h_freq=: 30 Hz, high edge
- =tmin=: 1 s post-onset, start of the feature window
- =tmax=: 2 s post-onset, end of it
- =montage=: standard electrode layout, resolved by =make_standard_montage=
- =fir_design=: =firwin=, forwarded to =Raw.filter=

** =DEFAULT=

The default instance, and the config =train= persists into the model file.

** =EVENT_ID=

Event codes, pinned: T1 -> 1, T2 -> 2. Pinned rather than inferred so the mapping cannot shift with the annotation order of a given run.

** =load_raw(subject, runs, *, path)=

Resolves the group's EDF paths, reads each, and concatenates them into one =Raw=. Returns it unfiltered, which is also what the "before" panel of the PSD figure needs.

** =preprocess(raw, config)=

Copies, standardises channel names, attaches the montage, band-passes. The copy is what lets [[file:../visualise.py][visualise.py]] hold raw and filtered side by side.

** =make_epochs(filtered, config)=

Picks EEG channels, reads events via =events_from_annotations= under the pinned =EVENT_ID=, and cuts the =tmin=/=tmax= window around each.

** =_labels_from_events(epochs)=

Shifts the event codes 1/2 down to labels 0/1.

** =load_dataset(subject, runs, config, *, path)=

The three calls above in order. Returns =X= (epochs as array), =y= (labels), and the =Epochs= object for figures.

* [[file:../src/csp.py][csp.py]]

Heart of project. A from scratch implementation of CSP.

** =MyCSP(BaseEstimator, TransformerMixin)=

Inherits for the sklearn contract, not for behaviour: =BaseEstimator= supplies the =get_params=/=set_params= that let =cross_val_score= clone the step, =TransformerMixin= supplies =fit_transform= for =Pipeline=. See the sklearn entry under Externals.

*** =__init__(n_components, log)=

Stores both parameters unchanged. No computation, no validation — the sklearn contract requires =__init__= to assign its arguments and nothing else, so the checks live in =fit=.

*** =_covariance(X)=

Reshapes the 3D tensor from =(epochs, channels, time)= to =(channels, epochs * time)= by transposition and flattening, concatenating the time-series of all epochs, then forms the covariance. Never centres — see "Preprocessing decisions" above and "Concatenation estimator" in [[file:csp.org][csp.org]].

*** =fit(X, y)=

Validates that =n_components= is even and that exactly two classes are present, then estimates =S_0= and =S_1= and solves =eigh(S_0, S_0 + S_1)= (see "Why =eigh= is the right solver"). Stores the eigenvalues descending in =self.eigvals_= and the corresponding eigenvectors as rows of =self.filters_= (see "Convention warning"). This is the whole of CSP: everything downstream is a projection onto those rows.

*** =transform(X)=

Guards on =check_is_fitted=, then projects each epoch onto the outermost =n_components/2= filter pairs — the extremes of the spectrum, where the variance ratio between the two classes is largest — and returns the per-component variance, shape =(n_epochs, n_components)=. If =log=, takes the log of those variances: they are heavy-tailed by construction, and LDA is linear, so the compression is what makes them separable by it.

* [[file:../src/train.py][train.py]]

Where training happens.

** =FitConfig=

Default split and estimator parameters, shared by =train= and the sweep.

- =n_components=: filters kept by =transform=, half from each end of the spectrum. Even by construction; =fit= raises otherwise.
- =test_size=: the holdout fraction, and the validation fraction inside each cv split
- =n_splits=: number of cross-validation folds
- =seed=: =random_state= for both the holdout split and the cv object

** =model_path(subject, runs)=

Builds the per-group model path under =MODELS_DIR=. Written by =train=, read by =predict=.

** =make_pipeline(n_components)=

Returns =Pipeline([MyCSP, LDA])=. Exists so the two call sites cannot drift apart.

** =fit_pipeline(X, y, fit, *, cross_validate)=

Splits =idx= — not =X= — into =train_idx= and =test_idx=, stratified, at =fit.seed=. Builds the pipeline, optionally cross-validates it on the training rows only, then fits it on those rows. Returns =clf=, =train_idx=, sorted =test_idx=, and =scores= (=None= when =cross_validate= is off, which is the sweep's case).

** =train(subject, runs, config, fit, *, verbose)=

Loads the dataset, fits through =fit_pipeline=, and dumps the payload to =model_path(...)=. =predict= reads only =pipeline=, =config=, =test_idx= and =len(train_idx)=; the rest is provenance. =verbose= is off when the sweep calls it. Returns =scores.mean()=.

* [[file:../src/predict.py][predict.py]]

Where a persisted model meets its held-out partition.

** =score_stream(clf, X, y, test_idx, *, verbose)=

Predicts one epoch at a time, timing each call, so the per-chunk bound the spec asks for is measured rather than asserted. Compares predictions to truths and returns the mean. Under =verbose= it also prints the table the spec lays out.

** =predict(subject, runs, *, verbose)=

Checks the model file exists, loads it, and reloads the dataset under the persisted =config= rather than the current default. Verifies the index range against the reloaded labels, so a model can never be scored against a different set of epochs. Runs =score_stream= and returns its accuracy.

* [[file:../src/sweep.py][sweep.py]]

The 109-subject evaluation. This is what discharges the 60% requirement.

** =N_REPEATS=

Holdout draws averaged per cell. A cell holds out ~9 of ~45 epochs, so one draw is quantised to 1/9 and depends heavily on which epochs it took. Five draws shrink that; they share the same epochs, so the gain is smaller than independent sampling would give.

** =evaluate(subject, runs, config, fit, *, n_repeats)=

Loads epochs and labels once for all repeats. Per repeat: derives a seed from =(fit.seed, subject, repeat)=, fits with =cross_validate=False=, scores with =verbose=False=. Returns the mean over repeats.

** =run_full_sweep()=

Iterates =EXPERIMENTS= over =SUBJECTS= on defaults, printing each cell, then the per-experiment mean, then the mean across experiments. Returns the per-experiment means.

Each cell is wrapped in =try/except=, so one bad subject cannot kill the run; failures are printed at the end and the experiment mean is taken over successful cells only, hence the =n== in the output.

* [[file:../mybci.py][mybci.py]]

An entrypoint of the project.

** =parse_args(argv)=

Defines the flags and their constraints, parses, then validates twice more: =subject= against =SUBJECTS=, and =run= by delegating to =runs_for=, whose =ValueError= is surfaced as an argparse usage error. Stores the resolved group in =args.runs=.

** =main(argv)=

Branches on the parsed arguments: no =subject= runs the full sweep, otherwise =mode= selects =train= or =predict= on =(args.subject, args.runs)=.

* [[file:../visualise.py][visualise.py]]

The PSD figure. Off the training path entirely.

** =parse_args(argv)=

Same validation as [[file:../mybci.py][mybci.py]], without =mode=.

** =plot_psd_comparison(raw, filtered, config)=

Clips =fmax= to Nyquist, which matters only for the four 128 Hz subjects. Draws the raw and filtered PSDs into two stacked axes, marks the band edges, then unions the y-limits across both so the stopband depth is readable. Returns =fig=.

** =main(argv)=

Parses, loads =raw=, preprocesses to =filtered=, builds the figure and writes it to =figs/=. Returns the path.
