#+title: Eegmmidb

* Format

EEGMMIDB gives each subject 14 runs. Runs 1-2 are the baselines (eyes open/closed, no T1/T2). The remaining 12 are 4 tasks times 3 repetitions:


|-------+-----------+----------+------------+------------|
| Group | Runs      | Task     | T1         | T2         |
|-------+-----------+----------+------------+------------|
| G0    | 3, 7, 11  | executed | left fist  | right fist |
|-------+-----------+----------+------------+------------|
| G1    | 4, 8, 12  | imagined | left fist  | right fist |
|-------+-----------+----------+------------+------------|
| G2    | 5, 9, 13  | executed | both fists | both feet  |
|-------+-----------+----------+------------+------------|
| G3    | 6, 10, 14 | imagined | both fists | both feet  |
|-------+-----------+----------+------------+------------|
